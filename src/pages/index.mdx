# Welcome to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Textbook

This is the interactive textbook for learning Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics: From Digital Intelligence to Embodied Humanoid Agents.

## Getting Started

- Start with the [Introduction](./docs/intro/introduction-to-physical-ai) to understand the scope and structure
- Proceed through the four modules in sequence to build your knowledge systematically
- Complete the capstone project to demonstrate your understanding
